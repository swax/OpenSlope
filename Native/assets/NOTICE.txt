Stock snowboard art
Copyright 2026 OpenSlope contributors
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Native/assets/snowboard.glb is baked first-party Slopesmith stock snowboard
geometry, exported through its public gear API by Native/tools/export-snowboard.ts.
The model has a half turn about Y to use Native's model-space nose convention.
Native builds embed this saved asset and do not link the authoring runtime.

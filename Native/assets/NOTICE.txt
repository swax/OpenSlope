Slopesmith avatar art

Native embeds the original Slopesmith/public/characters/*-rigged.glb files:
Blocky Rider, Kestrel, Robin, Raven, Alpine Exo, Servo Scout and Stick Figure.
The catalogue and assets are read directly from Slopesmith during the build.
Native's loader, pose driver and renderer are independently authored.
The older Native/assets/stick-figure-rigged.glb remains a reference asset.

Stock snowboard art
Copyright 2026 OpenSlope contributors
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Native/assets/snowboard.glb is baked first-party Slopesmith stock snowboard
geometry, exported through its public gear API by Native/tools/export-snowboard.ts.
The model has a half turn about Y to use Native's model-space nose convention.
Native builds embed this saved asset and do not link the authoring runtime.

The Contour, Sunset Rally, Timberline and Night Shift snowboard graphics are
original OpenSlope artwork, generated and approved for this project. Their shared
source PNGs are Slopesmith/public/equipment/boards/*.png. Native embeds them for
desktop and OpenXR playback. See Slopesmith/docs/stock-board-art.md for provenance.

Quest Touch Plus controller art
Copyright 2026 OpenSlope contributors
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0.

Native/assets/quest-touch-plus.glb is baked first-party Slopesmith controller
geometry, exported through its public controller art API by
Native/tools/export-controllers.mjs. It retains the source's OpenXR grip frame.
The left controller mirrors this right-hand asset through X. No Meta reference
mesh or texture is included. Native's input and finger posing are independent.

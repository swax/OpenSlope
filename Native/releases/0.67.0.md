# OpenSlope Native 0.67.0 — course contact preview

Author, convert and ride portable OpenSlope courses on Windows or standalone Quest.
This experimental preview improves the shared riding physics and optional opponents.

- Fixes repeated landings on curved terrain and at sloped floor/wall junctions.
- Keeps airborne riders moving after wall contacts and retains rail motion where terrain crosses a rail.
- Adds contact-driven recovery for sustained prop traps during scored runs, with clear recovery positions for opponents.
- Preserves opponents' forward route tracking when re-selecting their current path.

Desktop and Quest use the same corrected physics. Existing maps work without re-importing.
Ordinary impacts retain control; walking and freeride do not enable automatic wedge recovery.
The default opponent count remains zero; enable opponents in the desktop or wrist menu.

The Windows ZIP starts through **Play OpenSlope.cmd**. Add custom courses under `Maps/NAME`,
or use **Import courses.cmd** with a supported local ISO. The Quest APK can be sideloaded;
its launch library imports an ISO on the headset or opens an existing Maps folder.
Keep the same signing channel for in-place updates, and preserve your maps before deliberately
switching between debug and release signing. Uninstalling can remove app-owned data.

Full-course tests finish six opponents on eight of nine courses; one Alaska rider still circles
below its route near the finish. Gameplay parity, multiplayer, wider device coverage and
worn-VR performance validation remain in progress.
No disc images, extracted courses, commercial music or other retail media are included.
See `Native/docs/releases.md` for the manual release workflow and installation/signing details.

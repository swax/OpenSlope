# OpenSlope Native 0.87.0

- Adds four shared stock snowboard designs: Contour, Sunset Rally, Timberline and Night Shift.
- Game startup, course restarts and new-board pulls choose a random design without an immediate repeat. Respawning, catching and remounting keep the current board.
- Avatars and boards respond to course ambient tint and directional lights in normal lighting mode. Mounted, held and loose boards sample lighting at their own positions.

The same board designs and lighting are available in Slopesmith. Existing courses work without re-importing. Quest app updates retain maps and settings.

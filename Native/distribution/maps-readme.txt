Put custom maps here as Maps/NAME/Patches.json, with their other files beside it.
Click Refresh in the map library, then choose a course to play.

To import courses, put your SSX Tricky PS2 ISO beside OpenSlope.exe and choose
ISO Sources in the app. Click Extract; the button becomes Play when ready.
Maps stay here and work without the ISO. Existing files are preserved.

No game content is bundled. Keep imported disc content private.
